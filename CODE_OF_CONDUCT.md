# Tactevra community code of conduct

Tactevra welcomes people of different backgrounds, identities, abilities and
experience levels. Participation should be respectful, constructive and free
from harassment. These expectations apply to contributors and maintainers alike.

## How we work together

- Discuss the work, not a person's worth or identity. Disagree with reasoning
  and evidence; avoid insults, intimidation and repeated unwanted contact.
- Help newcomers ask questions, acknowledge mistakes and correct misleading
  claims. Technical criticism is welcome when expressed respectfully.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, share sexualized material or direct
  unwanted sexual attention at participants.
- Do not expose someone's private information without permission or retaliate
  against someone for reporting a concern or participating in its review.

This policy applies in project-managed spaces, including GitHub issues, pull
requests and reviews, and when officially representing Tactevra. It does not
give maintainers authority over unrelated communities or private disputes.

## Report a concern privately

The responsible maintainer is **[j-webtek](https://github.com/j-webtek)**.
Email **[iowebtek@gmail.com](mailto:iowebtek@gmail.com)** with the subject
`Tactevra conduct report`. This address is published with the owner's permission.

Describe what happened, relevant dates and links, and how you would prefer to be
contacted. Include only information needed to understand the concern. Do not
post reports or personal details in public issues; do not send passwords, raw
device backups or unrelated private material. A proposed remedy is optional.

This is a manually handled mailbox, not an emergency service. No response time
or continuous monitoring is promised. Acknowledgment confirms receipt, not an
investigation outcome. The publication of this address does not establish that
delivery has been tested.

## Review and response

The maintainer reviews reports in context, seeks relevant clarification and
considers the people affected. Information should be shared only as needed to
review and respond; absolute confidentiality or anonymity cannot be guaranteed.
Reporters may raise concerns about disclosure before providing further details.

Depending on severity, repetition and impact, responses may include asking for
a correction, a warning, removing harmful content, or temporarily or permanently
restricting participation in project-managed spaces. Serious incidents need not
follow a fixed warning sequence. Reasons should be explained to affected people
when appropriate, without publishing private report details.

You may request reconsideration through the same address and provide additional
context. No separate independent reviewer is currently designated. If a report
concerns j-webtek or another conflict of interest, state that concern; the
maintainer should disclose the conflict and seek an uninvolved reviewer where
available, not claim an independent process that does not exist.

## Other kinds of reports

For vulnerabilities, exposed secrets or execution-gate bypasses, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For ordinary bugs, documentation questions and feature
requests, use [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Conduct review does not authorize firmware
installation, robot movement or release publication.

## Policy reference

This is a project-specific policy, informed by the
[Contributor Covenant's community standards](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It is not an unmodified adoption of that template. Reporting arrangements and
policy changes are maintained through repository review.
