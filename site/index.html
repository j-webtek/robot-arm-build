Tactevra

Physical intelligence, checked.

Tactevra is an offline-first control architecture for robotics teams building AI-assisted physical interfaces: models propose actions, deterministic software decides what may move.

Status: Proposal contracts, deterministic gates, calibration records, and the controller boundary are implemented project software. Arm motion and key contact shown here are architectural simulations, not physical qualification.
