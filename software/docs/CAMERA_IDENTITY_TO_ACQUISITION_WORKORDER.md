## Deliverable

The operator can continue from the final original USB identity review to:

1. Enter camera mode/control setup without opening the camera.
2. Inspect and review the exact supported acquisition runtime.
3. Explicitly run a finite capability probe of the selected camera.
4. Choose an advertised format and supported controls, then compare requested
   settings with native readback.
5. Explicitly acquire a bounded image and see its age, dimensions, quality and
   settings identity in the existing wizard.
6. Stop, inspect failures and export all retained diagnostics to the assigned
   folder; reopen the original later without reconnecting or replaying.

Stages 5 and 6 cover mode/controls and frame freshness. Optical calibration,
board registration, arm startup, motion and contact remain separate stages.
A preview image must never be presented as calibrated coordinates or permission
to move. The initial feature is a finite, closed capture, not a permanent stream.

## Slice D — useful setup guidance

- Overlay nominal board bounds separately from measured registration. Mark
  uncalibrated overlays clearly; no screen pixel is yet a robot coordinate.
- Guide physical focus with center/corner crops and consistent sharpness checks;
  exposure, clipping, lighting and field coverage get their own observations.
- Treat repeated image content carefully: an unchanged static board can produce
  identical pixels. Freshness uses acquisition/timestamp evidence, not a rule
  that every image hash must differ. Flag implausible timing separately.
- Camera support, lens, resolution, crop, rotation or focus changes invalidate
  the affected calibration candidates. Do not rescale an old calibration and
  mark it measured without validation.
- Leave stages 7/8 and arm stages 9–12 visibly incomplete until their original
  acquisition/review workflows exist. No keyboard or phone motion is unlocked
  by a successful camera picture.

Keep fast tests separate from the long original-history public acceptance.
During a long run, do not edit fingerprinted source/configuration/native files
or run competing heavy tests; observation timeouts are not test failures.

| Lane | Required evidence | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pure contract tests | Exact stage entry, wrong predecessor/identity/epoch rejection, fixed caps, all partial writes, unchanged legacy acceptance | Original storage or device I/O |
| Composed service/UI | Actual routing, action fields, no automatic effects, stale tickets, publication ordering, both validators, Stop/fault display | Windows storage durability or native camera behavior |

| Received-hardware lane | Exact device/driver, advertised modes, settings readback, frame timing/content, exclusive ownership and cleanup | Board/arm calibration or contact safety |

unsupported format, readback mismatch, malformed/oversized result, missing frame,
source/epoch change, Stop before/after release, failed cleanup, partial original
publication, export failure and reopening every terminal outcome without replay.
