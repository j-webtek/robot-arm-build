# Storyboard v2 image authority

Use the `*-layout-accurate.png` frames as geometry references for current
review and advertising handoff. They are reproducible composites generated by
`../build_storyboard_layout_frames.py` from
`source/workcell-layout-authoritative.png`.

The source is a render of the repository Blender workcell and contains the
actual presentation assets built by `../build_workcell_explainer.py`:

- 610 × 457 × 18 mm RC03 board;
- measured 315 × 147 × 21 mm keyboard and its RC03 station geometry;
- measured 77.9 × 164.4 × 7.9 mm phone and RC03 phone-station geometry;
- rear-center robot transform;
- released board fiducials and corner hardware.

The cards, colored bounds, route marks, and phone screen states are storyboard
graphics. They explain intended behavior and are not measurements or evidence
of completed physical operation. The v2.1 film requires a new dynamic shot
palette; these overhead composites do not replace its six art-direction stills.

The original five image-generated advertising studies remain in this folder
for historical comparison. Do not use their incidental hardware geometry.
`reference-only/visual-treatment-reference.png` records the most recent
image-generated lighting study; it is explicitly not a placement reference.

To rebuild the current frames:

```powershell
python presentations/blender/build_storyboard_layout_frames.py
```
